    Episode #362: Seth Stephens-Davidowitz, Data Scientist & Author, “You Do Have To Be Willing To Go Where The Data Takes You”

     

    Guest: Seth Stephens-Davidowitz is a data scientist, author, and keynote speaker. His book, Everybody Lies: Big Data, New Data, and What the Internet Can Tell Us About Who We Really Are, was a New York Times bestseller and an Economist Book of the Year. Seth has worked as a data scientist at Google and a visiting lecturer at the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania. He is currently a contributing op-ed writer for the New York Times. His next book, Don’t Trust Your Gut: Using Data to Get What You Really Want in Life, is scheduled to release in Spring 2022.

    Date Recorded: 9/29/2021     |     Run-Time: 48:47

Seth walks us through the takeaways for finding a spouse, living healthier, and being happier.

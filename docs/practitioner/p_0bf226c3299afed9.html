4 October 2024

Simon Judes in conversation with Bloomberg

Winton Co-CIO Simon Judes spoke to Erik Schatzker at the Bloomberg Hedge Fund Forum in London.

Winton's Simon Judes spoke to Erik Schatzker at the Bloomberg Hedge Fund Forum in London about the state of the quantitative investment management industry.

Topics covered included the development of Winton's multi-strategy programme, the growing adoption of trend-following CTAs by large asset allocators, London as a location for quant talent, and the benefits of a large and collaborative research team.

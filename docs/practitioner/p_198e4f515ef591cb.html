Quarterly Update Service

Skip to content

This service provides quarterly email updates of selected spreadsheets that you have purchased from Six Figure Investing.  The updates will be sent out around the second week of January, April, July, and October.  The spreadsheets will be updated to at least the last trading day of the previous quarter, any splits / reverse splits will be accounted for, and any unusual events (e.g., unscheduled market holidays like December 5th, 2018) dealt with. 

The charge for this service is $15 per quarter.  Unfortunately, PayPal does not support a quarterly recurring payment schedule so you’ll see a $5 charge each month even though you only receive an update once a quarter.

When I receive your initial subscription I will send you the latest version that I currently have of your spreadsheet within a couple of days.  I support quarterly updates for the following spreadsheets:

  • VIX Futures Lookup
  • VIX Futures Lookup and Volatility Index Calculations
  • ETP End-of-Day Settlement Prices (VXX, VIXY,UVXY, SVXY,VIXM, VXZ)
  • ETP Open-High-Low-Close Prices (VXX, VIXY, UVXY, SVXY, VIXM)  The Open-High-Low data updates past October 2013 will be based on publically available trade data. 

If you’ve purchased a spreadsheet from me that is not on this list and you’d like to get quarterly updates please send me an email at [email protected] and I’ll consider adding it to the list of supported spreadsheets. 

The service can be canceled at any time. If requested, any fees paid in the current quarter will be refunded after cancellation.

Welton Global ranked among the top-25 hedge funds by Barron’s

by Welton Investment Partners | Jun 20, 2017 | News, Articles, & PR

Welton Global formerly known as Welton Global Directional Portfolio (GDP) ranked #22 among global hedge funds for outstanding annualized three-year compounded performance*. Read the full article here. Barron’s Penta rankings of hedge fund managers are based on data compiled by Barron’s and information supplied by hedge fund research firms, hedge funds and investors.

A complete list of all 100 managers is available here.

*Barron’s Penta Best 100 Hedge Funds list ranks investment programs based on annualized three-year compound performance.  There are no fees charged by the organizer for a manager to participate in the selection process. Research begins with information provided by three major hedge fund databases: BarclayHedge, Morningstar, and Preqin.  They also rely on industry contacts and other sources they deem reliable to report on firms that don’t file with the databases.  Funds must have at least $300 million in assets and a minimum 3-year track record as of the prior ending calendar year.  The list screens out narrow industries and small regions, excludes funds that invest in only one sector or country, and avoids commodity-focused funds.  Gold or energy funds are omitted, but diversified commodity trading advisors, i.e., diversified managed futures, are considered.

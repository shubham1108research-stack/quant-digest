    Episode #297: Tim Ranzetta, Next Gen Personal Finance, “Every Student Wants To Learn About Money”

     

    Guest: Tim Ranzetta is the co-founder of Next Gen Personal Finance (NGPF), a non-profit organization to partner with teachers by sharing timely and relevant curricular resources, providing effective professional development, and advocating to increase access to financial education.

    Date Recorded: 3/3/2021     |     Run-Time: 1:17:21

    Summary: In today’s episode, we start the episode with the current state of K-12 personal finance education in the U.S. Then we hear how teaching a personal finance class in a school inspired Tim to start Next Gen Personal Finance in 2014. He shares the curriculum they provide to teachers for free, why the 43,000 teachers that are registered on the Next Gen platform are the champions to get personal finance mandated in schools across America, and how he utilizes arcade games to engage kids ranging from K-12.

    As we wind down, we hear about what Tim calls Mission 2030, which is to get every student who graduates high school to complete a one-semester personal finance course.

If there’s an upward trend, go long. If there’s a downward trend, go short. Keep your risk parameters under control. Those are the basic rules of trend following. They are intuitive and can be implemented systematically; the application is the trickier bit. We think all investors should be well informed on what systematic trend following is and the benefits an allocation can offer as a core component of a well-diversified portfolio. In this piece, we explain how systematic trend following can work in practice

Free Bitcoin/Bitcoin ETF/Bitcoin Futures/GBTC Quotes, Charts, & Historical Data

Skip to content

Finding quotes and historical data for Bitcoin, Bitcoin futures, Bitcoin options can be an adventure.  Below I’ve assembled links to the online resources that I’ve been able to find.

In many cases, data is available from multiple sources.  I did not attempt to list all of them.

BitCoin Futures based ETFs

  • Proshares BITO  Will primarliy hold the front month futures
    • BITO Prospectus
  • Valkyrie BTF
    • Prospectus

BTC Quotes

  • CME Real Time Index (BRTI)
    • CME  Methodology
  • Yahoo Finance (BTC-USD)
    • Does not identify which exchange (s) it is using
    • Shows market cap, supply, volume statistics
  • Gemini Exchange
  • Coinbase (on right, shows bid / ask)

BTC volume and “Market Cap” information

  • CoinGecko
  • Bitcoinity

BTC Charts

  • Bitcoincharts
  • Investing.com

 BTC Historical Prices

  • Yahoo Finance (BTC-USD)
    • Daily data (including open-high-close and volume) since 1-Jan-2010
  • Gemini Auction
  • Blockchain  (daily for 2 years)

BTC Futures    

  • How do Bitcoin Futures Work?
  • CME  Symbol: BTC
    • Quotes
      • Spot:  Bitcoin Real Time Index (BRTI)  methodology
      • CME Futures
        • All months
    • Open Interest 
    • Reference exchanges:  Bitstamp, GDAX, itBit, Kraken (combined into a composite quote via BRTI & BRR)
    • Contract Size:  5 Bitcoins, cash settled  (Contract Specifications)
    • Trading Hours:  Trading started 18-Dec-2017
       
      CME Globex:Sunday – Friday 6:00 p.m. – 5:00 p.m.  ET (5:00 p.m. – 4:00 p.m. CT) with a 60-minute break each day beginning at 5:00 p.m. ET (4:00 p.m. CT)
      CME ClearPort:Sunday – Friday 6:00 p.m. – 5:00 p.m.  ET(5:00 p.m. – 4:00 p.m. CT) with a 60-minute break each day beginning at 5:00 p.m. (4:00 p.m. CT
    • Future settlement quote: BRR (once per day)  methodology) )   Historical Data

BTC Mutual Funds

  • GBTC  Greyscale Bitcoin Investment Trust  (A closed-end mutual fund)  Available for trading in regular brokerage accounts and IRAs
    • Quotes Bloomberg  Yahoo 
    • Historical Data  Yahoo
    • Tracking Error with BTC
      • Can get quite large.

BTC Options & Swaps

  • Proshares BITO
  • LedgerX offers Bitcoin settled options
    • Their options are “physically” settled with Bitcoin
    •  To participate if appears you must be an institution or a high net worth ($10 million+) individual
    • See this page for a currently listed options and swaps

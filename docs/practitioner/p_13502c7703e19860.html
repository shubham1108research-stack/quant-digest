Our second full year of being a certified B Corporation has been characterised by a continued focus on a number of ongoing CSR initiatives as well as incremental enhancements to our practices to align with objectives originally identified during the certification process itself. Among the key benefits we identified when we first embarked upon the B Impact Assessment back in 2022 were validation that our existing initiatives and practices were in line with industry best practice, and identifying areas for improvement; and it therefore makes sense that the initial post-certification phase of celebrating our success in becoming accredited has given way to a period of consolidation and incremental improvements as we gear up towards re-certification in 2025.

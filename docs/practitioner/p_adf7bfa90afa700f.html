Protecting Portfolios with Diversifying Strategies

In-Person Conference

As recent regime shifts rattle global markets, the role of hedge funds in an institutional portfolio rises in importance and value. Increasingly, risk mitigation is widely revered as one of the key hallmarks of these diversifying strategies. Creativity abounds as this solutions-oriented culture gains steam and hedge funds are relied upon for both offense and defense in answer to the needs of sophisticated investors.

Join us for substantive discussions between institutional asset owners, allocators, asset managers, solutions providers, and academics who will gather to opine on complex investment challenges and turn a spotlight on fresh solutions designed to optimize outcomes for investors.

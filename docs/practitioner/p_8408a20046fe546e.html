Step by Step: Trend Following

Video

21 March 2024

Trend following is reactive, not predictive. The system isn’t trying to predict the beginning or the end of a trend, but rather to identify whether a trend exists, the direction of that trend and when to enter and exit the trend. But how does trend following actually work? In this short video we talk through the step-by-step process for a typical systematic trend following strategy.

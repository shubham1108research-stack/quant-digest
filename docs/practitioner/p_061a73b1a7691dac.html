Welton Global Awarded Top Performer status by Investors Choice Awards Americas 2020

by Craig Oliphant | Jun 29, 2020 | News, Articles, & PR

We are pleased to announce that Welton Global has been awarded  “Top Performer” status in the Managed Futures Fund Category of the Investors Choice Awards Americas 2020 sponsored by Allocator.

The annual Investors Choice Awards honor fund managers that have achieved outstanding risk adjusted absolute returns. All funds which are kept up to date on the Allocator platform are considered for an award nomination. There are no fees charged by the organizer for a manager to participate in the selection process. The Top Performer Awards are granted to the select few funds which have outperformed their wider peer group in each category. Winners are determined purely based on quantitative risk adjusted returns. For 2020 Allocator extended the time period under consideration to evaluate how managers navigated both 2019 and the Covid-19 related market turbulence in early 2020. The 2020 Top Performer award winners were chosen based on absolute returns from 1 Jan 2019 to 30 April 2020. The judges consist of key members of the investment teams of leading institutional investors and asset management firms.

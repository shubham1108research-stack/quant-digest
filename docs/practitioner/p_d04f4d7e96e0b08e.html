Total Portfolio Approach

The total portfolio approach to investing (TPA) has been aptly described as "buzzy but fuzzy." 1 1 Close FT Alphaville, November 2025.   Many commentators have tried to sort the substance from the hype: in this article we bring a skeptical but constructive and distinctively quantitative lens to that task. We identify a set of tangible advantages and a countervailing set of possible risks from removing traditional strategic asset allocation constraints or guardrails, and we offer four practical demonstrations of how a total portfolio mindset can help to drive long-term success for investors with many different objectives and approaches, whatever name they give to their investment framework.

AQR has been recognized as one of Pensions & Investments’ Best Places to Work in Money Management for 2022, marking the sixth consecutive year that the firm has received this distinction.

In the employee survey that serves as part of the criteria for the recognition, employees shared what makes AQR such a special place to work. They highlighted how AQR genuinely cares for its employees, citing the firm’s benefits and hybrid work model. Responses also noted the importance of being surrounded by smart, supportive colleagues and the availability of ongoing learning opportunities.

AQR was named one of Pensions and Investment’s Best Places to Work in Money Management from 2017 - 2022.

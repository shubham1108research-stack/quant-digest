Welton named as a category finalist in Institutional Investor’s 20th Annual Hedge Fund Industry Awards

We are honored to share that Welton Investment Partners has been named among six finalists within the category of “Quantitative Hedge Fund Manager of the Year” in Institutional Investor’s 20th Annual Hedge Fund Industry Awards.

We extend our hearty congratulations to the distinguished list of fellow finalists listed below.

  • AQR Capital Management
  • Aspect Capital
  • Graham Capital Management
  • PGIM Wadhwani
  • Systematica Investments
  • Welton Investment Partners

Winners will be determined by a vote of U.S. institutional investors, and announced on May 11, 2023 at the Mandarin Oriental in New York.

 

 

*Institutional Investor’s Annual Hedge Fund Industry Awards recognizes the hedge funds, funds of hedge funds, investment consultants, endowments, foundations, family offices, corporate funds, public funds, sovereign funds and rising stars that stood out for their performance innovation, achievements and contributions to the industry in the past year. There are no fees charged by the organizer for a manager to participate in the selection process. Finalist selection is based on the criteria and process set forth below by the organizer and may not reflect the opinions of all investors and industry experts. Past consideration for such an award is not necessarily indicative of future qualifications. Following a public call for nominations, the editorial staff of Institutional Investor selects award nominees based on how strongly candidates — both those put forward via the call for nominations and those independently identified by the editorial staff — meet the criteria for their respective categories. Once the nominees are publicly announced, the group then conducts a wide survey of U.S. institutional investors and invites them to vote for the manager nominees. Institutional Investor’s editorial staff analyzes the results of the voting to determine the winners.

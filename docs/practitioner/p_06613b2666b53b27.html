Anthony Todd, Co-Founder and CEO, joins Top Traders Unplugged for an insightful discussion on the role of systematic trading and trend following in today’s economy. He also discusses what has driven Aspect Capital’s shift towards developing multiple strategies and how they guide clients to make better investment decisions, whether the trend following industry should be concerned about replication strategies and how allocations are funded today. Lastly, the conversation touches on how Aspect Capital uses AI and how systematic trading has changed as a whole with the rise of AI, what Anthony is most excited and concerned about in 2024 and much more.

    Episode #318: Perth Tolle, Life + Liberty Indexes, “If I Was In Hong Kong Right Now, I Would Be Arrested, Definitely, For Doing This”

     

    Guest: Perth Tolle is the founder of Life + Liberty Indexes. Prior to forming Life + Liberty Indexes, Perth was a private wealth advisor at Fidelity Investments in Los Angeles and Houston.

    Date Recorded: 5/5/2021     |     Run-Time: 57:37

    Summary: In today’s episode, we’re talking about freedom! Perth shares what led her to focus on the relationship between freedom and investing in emerging markets. We hear the story of how she launched the ETF with the help of former podcast guests Rob Arnott and Wes Gray. She shares the inner workings of her Freedom Index and how it uses personal and economic freedom metrics as factors.

You can actually… I could pick and choose any of the 76 to use, but I don’t. I use the composite score so that I have a comprehensive measure of the freedom in each of the countries and I’m not putting any of my subjective opinion and picking and choosing any particular one.

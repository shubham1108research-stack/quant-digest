Location:  Omni Austin Hotel Downtown, 700 San Jacinto at East 8th Street, Austin, TX 78701

Event Topic: The Positive Impact of Risk-Mitigating Alternative Strategies

An increased focus on risk-mitigating alternative strategies has led to a sea change in portfolio construction and asset allocation. We’ll talk about the abundance of innovation and customization in the alternative investment industry designed to meet the needs of asset owners and fiduciaries as they strive to protect and grow their portfolios in a volatile world.

Join us for substantive discussions between institutional investors, allocators, managers, solutions providers, and academics who will analyze the investment landscape and discuss fresh solutions designed to optimize investor outcomes.

Welton Proudly Accepts the SBAI’s Invitation to Join as a Core Supporter

by Guillaume Detrait | Aug 4, 2023 | News, Articles, & PR

Welton Investment Partners is pleased to join the select group of the Core Supporters of the Standard Board for Alternative Investments (SBAI). This group consists of institutional investors and asset managers, and plays a more proactive role in the SBAI’s initiatives to support its mission to improve industry outcomes.

Welton is a long-time signatory to the SBAI dating back to 2013, which was then known as the Hedge Fund Standards Board (HFSB). Welton values the SBAI’s mission to improve industry outcomes by setting standards, providing guidance, and facilitating collaboration and an exchange of ideas among its 250 signatories and Investor Chapter Members responsible for US$ 8 trillion in assets. We are now pleased to be among the Core Supporters committed to helping shape and facilitate solutions for our industry’s benefit.

As part of Welton’s increased commitment, the firm is expanding staff participation from one to three individuals across the following SBAI groups:

  • Operational Leaders Community
    • Todd Merrell, Chief Financial Officer
  • Responsible Investment Working Group
    • Dr. François Chevallier, Research Strategist, Machine Learning Strategies
  • Culture & Diversity Working Group
    • Guillaume Detrait, President & Chief Risk Officer

“We are honored to join this distinguished group of thought leaders. In our 30+ years in business, we’ve witnessed our industry successfully evolve, in part, with the SBAI’s assistance. Today, we look forward to collaborating with our peers to help our industry address the next decade and beyond,” Guillaume Detrait, President and Chief Risk Officer.

A complete list of Core Supporters may be found at the bottom of this page on the SBAI homepage.

October 17-18, 2023

Location:  Marina de Rey Hotel, 13534 Bali Way, Marina del Rey, CA 90292

Event Topic: Customization & Alignment: Optimizing Hedge Fund Allocations

As institutional asset owners continue to pursue bespoke solutions for their portfolio needs, hedge funds and solutions providers accelerate their provision of innovative strategies and tactics. Modernized approaches to alignment of interests provide access to new possibilities that drive improvements in risk reduction and alpha creation.

Join us for substantive discussions between institutional investors, allocators, managers, solutions providers, and academics who will examine the current challenges and fresh solutions designed to optimize outcomes for investors.

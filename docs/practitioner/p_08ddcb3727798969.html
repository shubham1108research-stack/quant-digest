Winton joins Gulf industry group

GCMA works with governments, central banks and regulators to strengthen the region's markets.

Founded in 2010, the Gulf Capital Market Association (GCMA) harnesses the expertise and energy of its members to strengthen the capital markets needed to support economic growth and transformation of the Gulf region. Member firms are industry-leading issuers, asset managers, banks, investors, ratings agencies, legal advisors and market infrastructure providers.

At Winton, we have a history of bringing our expertise in quantitative investment management to bear early in emerging and alternative markets that are growing in global importance. By applying our 25 years of experience to Middle East capital markets, we hope to contribute positively to their continued development.

Winton Co-CIO on Top Traders Unplugged

Simon Judes describes our approach to trend following in a wide-ranging discussion.

Simon Judes was interviewed by Niels Kaastrup-Larsen and Alan Dunne on this week’s Top Traders Unplugged podcast. Recorded at the beginning of March 2023, many of Simon’s comments on risk management proved prescient, particularly around the risks to CTA strategies of short fixed income and long equity positions.

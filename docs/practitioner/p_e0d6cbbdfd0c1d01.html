Welton Featured in FA Magazine’s Four-Part Series on Alternative Investing

Welton Investment Partners was recently featured in a four-part FA Magazine series dissecting “The New Playbook for Alternative Investing” — a framework for helping advisors think more strategically about the role of alternatives in modern portfolios.

Using Welton’s football-inspired lens, the series explores how alternatives may contribute across four topic areas: 

  • Part 1: OFFENSE – Growth Strategies
  • Part 2: DEFENSE – Income Strategies
  • Part 3: SPECIAL TEAMS – Diversification
  • Part 4: COACHING THE PORTFOLIO – Putting It All Together

Together, the series highlights how advisors can move beyond a traditional 60/40 mindset and consider a more dynamic approach to diversification, resilience, and long-term investment planning.

Welton is grateful to FA Magazine for the recognition and for the opportunity to contribute to an important industry conversation around the evolving role of alternative investments.

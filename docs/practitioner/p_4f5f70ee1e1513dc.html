Welton Employee Running for The Leukemia & Lymphoma Society’s 2018 Man of the Year.

by Welton Investment Partners | Apr 9, 2018 | News, Articles, & PR

Welton would like to support and recognize Adam Roach for his candidacy for 2018 Man of the Year for the Leukemia & Lymphoma Society. Man & Woman of the Year candidates across the country compete in honor of a local boy and girl who are blood cancer survivors, to raise the most funds for blood cancer research. Every dollar counts as one vote, and the titles are awarded to the man and woman with the most votes at the end of the campaign. The top local fundraisers in the country win the national titles.

“We are extremely proud to support Adam in his campaign to support The Leukemia and Lymphoma Society and the groundbreaking research that they enable.  Adam embodies the societal values that we too hold dear, and we are inspired by his tireless commitment to this most worthy cause.  Please join us in supporting his goal!”

 Dr. Patrick Welton
Chief Executive Officer & Chief Investment Officer

Please help us in support of Adam as he tries to raise $50,000 by May 5, 2018. For more information about Adam’s campaign and direct donations please visit Adam’s fundraising page.

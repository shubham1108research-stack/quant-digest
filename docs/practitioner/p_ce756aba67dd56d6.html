AQR came away with two category wins and one “highly commended” recognition at the recently held With Intelligence HFM Performance Awards 2023, which recognize and reward the highest achieving hedge funds, CTAs and funds of hedge funds in the US.  

AQR’s Apex Strategy won the “Multi-strategy under $1 billion” category, and our Alternative Trends Strategy won the “Managed futures (CTA) long-term performance (5 years)” category and was highly commended in the “Managed futures (CTA) under $1bn” category. 

Winners were selected by a panel comprised of investors and investment consultants, who judged candidates based on prior one-year performance, longer-term track records and qualitative criteria.

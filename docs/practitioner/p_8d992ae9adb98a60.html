16 January 2024

Leading Practitioners in Systematic Investing

Winton Co-CIO makes annual EQDerivatives shortlist that recognises leaders in the field.

Winton’s Simon Judes was recognised by EQDerivatives in their 2023 list of Leading Practitioners in Volatility and Systematic Investing.

The annual list is compiled by EQDerivatives’ editorial team and highlights individuals that “made a significant contribution in terms of research, product design or strategy innovation in quantitative and volatility investing last year”.

Congratulations to Simon and everyone else who made the list.

Visit EQDerivatives (paywall) for further information.
